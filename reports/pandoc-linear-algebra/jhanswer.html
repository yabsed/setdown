Preface

These are answers to the exercises in Linear Algebra by J Hefferon. An answer labeled here as One.II.3.4 is for question 4 from the first chapter, second section, and third subsection. The Topics are numbered separately.

If you have an electronic version of this file then save it in the same directory as the book’s electronc file. Then clicking on the question number in the book takes you to its answer and clicking on the answer number takes you to the question. (The book’s file must be named ‘book.pdf’ and this answer file must be named ‘jhanswer.pdf’.)

I sometimes get reports that folks have bought a paper copy of the answers, and they don’t match the questions in their text. What’s happening is that because I give these away, there are people who download and sell them. These people do not care whether the copies they are selling are current. I can only suggest that you get the materials from me or from links that I give.

I welcome bug reports and comments, and gratefully include names in the Acknowledgements file. Contact information is on the book’s home page https://hefferon.net/linearalgebra.
