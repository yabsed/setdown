External PDF reader validation — 2026-10-31

Implementation
- Inline HTTP(S) PDF documents open in the existing Setdown PDF.js reader.
- Reads the committed top-level document MIME in an isolated JavaScript world.
  Filename extensions are irrelevant; attachments keep the download flow.
- Keeps URL, browser navigation history and the tab's canonical zoom.
- Local and external PDFs share the three-surface media LRU. Warm tab returns
  retain canvas/worker/search/scroll identity. The navigation token invalidates
  old surfaces and authorizes bounded IPC reads in the owning window.
- Browser-session GET downloads preserve cookies and redirects and use a private
  temporary immutable file. The cap is 256 MiB and the timeout is 120 seconds.
- Navigation/close cancels the load and deletes the snapshot. Save PDF copies
  the original bytes. Errors provide retry and the native browser viewer.
- Remote positions are navigation-scoped; they do not persist across restarts.
  One-time or POST-only URLs can require the original browser viewer.

  selectable title and body text, fit width 170% at a 1440 x 1000 window.
  Final result: arxiv-result.json, arxiv-final.log, arxiv-setdown.png.

  search/outline/zoom/canvas retention, keyboard and native-menu copy, byte-exact
  saving, stale range token rejection, Back, cookies, redirects, MIME/error
  handling, original downloads and real extension network effects.
  Log: focused-mime-fix.log.

  Replaced the session listener with committed-document MIME detection; all four
  focused scenarios then passed. Earlier diagnostic logs remain for provenance.

  Log: contract-complete.log. Captures/exports: contract-artifacts/.
