Setdown math SVG regression — 2026-11-03

Cause
The responsive media rule `.markdown-preview svg { max-width: 100%; height: auto; }`
overrode KaTeX's `height: inherit`. Underbrace SVGs have a 400000-by-548 viewBox;
treating that as an image aspect ratio collapses the curve while its text label remains.
An isolated Chromium reproduction measured 10.59375px inherited brace heights versus
0.015625–0.046875px with the Setdown media rule (default-size standalone fixture).

Change
Exclude SVGs inside .katex from responsive image sizing. KaTeX retains its calculated
height; authored SVG images retain their responsive sizing.
The fixture preserves the reported aligned equation and additionally covers overbrace,
radical, extensible arrow, and a wide authored SVG image.
The Electron regression checks real geometry and captured brace pixels in light/dark themes.
The original build failed this regression; the corrected build passed.
